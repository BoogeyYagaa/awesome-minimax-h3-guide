## Copy-ready prompt

```text
Create an overcast-weather planning shot at the location in Image 1 with the adult cast styling in Image 2. One actor walks from the right doorway to the covered bench over six seconds, then stops for two seconds. Soft diffuse skylight, dry ground and no rain. Preserve the doorway, bench, walking direction and clothing. Use a fixed wide camera. Footsteps and distant wind only. This is a proposed blocking plan, not evidence of actual weather or an event that occurred.
```
