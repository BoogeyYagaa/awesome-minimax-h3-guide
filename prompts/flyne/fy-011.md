## Copy-ready prompt

```text
Show the pet from Image 1 seated calmly on a rug, wearing the correctly fitted accessory from Image 2. Over six seconds the pet turns its head slightly toward an off-screen owner, blinks and returns to rest. Keep the camera fixed at the pet’s eye level. Preserve accessory width, buckle position and visible clearance from the fitted reference. Natural breathing and quiet indoor ambience. No pulling, jumping, eating, distress, size labels or claims that a generated image proves safe fit.
```

**Acceptance:** Owner checks likeness; fit must be validated physically, not from synthetic pixels.

**Recovery:** Reduce motion to a blink if the accessory slides or tightens.

**Production value:** Communicates a look while keeping real-world fit verification separate.
