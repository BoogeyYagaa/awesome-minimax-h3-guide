# Community conduct / 社区行为准则

Be respectful, specific and evidence-based. Critique prompts and results rather than people. Welcome contributors across languages and experience levels. Do not harass, disclose private information, spam, or submit deceptive claims or unlicensed media.

When a discussion becomes unproductive, maintainers may ask for revisions, remove content or restrict participation. Report ordinary issues through a repository issue; do not post private information in public reports.

尊重不同语言和经验的贡献者，用证据讨论内容与结果。禁止骚扰、泄露隐私、垃圾推广及故意误导。维护者可要求修改、移除内容或限制参与。
