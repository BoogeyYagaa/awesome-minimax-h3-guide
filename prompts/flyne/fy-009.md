## Copy-ready prompt

```text
Make a six-second calm event-screen background: two translucent teal fabric ribbons drift slowly across the far left and right margins of a dark blue frame. Keep the central 60 percent visually quiet with stable low-detail shading. No flashes, sudden cuts, camera movement, letters or high-frequency patterns. Slow smooth motion returns close to its initial arrangement. Silence. The empty center is reserved for high-contrast captions added during editing.
```

**Acceptance:** Test actual captions for readability on the venue screen; assess motion sensitivity with reviewers.

**Recovery:** Reduce motion or use a static background when it competes with text.

**Production value:** Supports readable presentation assets without claiming accessibility certification.

The duration is a creative target, not an API parameter guarantee. Select a compatible route in the [workflow guide](../../docs/workflows.md), record results with the [run template](../../templates/run-record.json), and verify input rights before use.
