## Copy-ready prompt

```text
Generate one six-second studio shot of the bag in Image 1. Use Image 2 solely for its blue material color. A fixed three-quarter camera observes the bag rotate 20 degrees on a concealed turntable, then stop for the final two seconds. Preserve seams, buckle count, strap width and silhouette. Neutral gray backdrop and soft top light; quiet turntable sound only. No text, added accessories or color morphing. Generate the ochre version as a separate run, replacing only the color-reference role with Image 3. Do not combine both variants in one clip.
```

**Acceptance:** Compare both outputs against the product reference; reject altered seams or hardware.

**Recovery:** Reduce rotation before relaxing the product constraints.

**Production value:** Creates comparable variants without a fabricated live color-changing product.

The duration is a creative target, not an API parameter guarantee. Select a compatible route in the [workflow guide](../../docs/workflows.md), record results with the [run template](../../templates/run-record.json), and verify input rights before use.
