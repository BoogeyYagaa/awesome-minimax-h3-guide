**Use it for:** A sports commercial centered on technique and one decisive movement.

**Mode:** Athlete reference + route image + technique video  
**Format:** 16:9, target 10 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = fictional adult athlete identity and kit; Image 2 = exact wall and hold colors; Video 1 = body mechanics only.

```text
Create a focused indoor climbing sequence. Preserve the adult athlete from Image 1: same face, braided hair, teal top, charcoal trousers, chalk bag, correctly fitted harness. Use the exact yellow route and wall geometry from Image 2. Video 1 supplies the heel-hook mechanics only.

Opening low side angle: athlete stabilizes on two holds and looks once at the final hold. Camera rises slightly as the right heel hooks the yellow volume, hips rotate inward, and the left hand releases without slipping. Cut to a front close view only after the body settles; athlete reaches dynamically with the left hand, catches the final hold, swings once under real gravity, then controls the swing and matches hands.

Sound intent: breath, shoe rubber, chalk, room reverb, one quiet crowd reaction. Preserve rope path, harness, hold positions, and route color. Avoid impossible reach, floating feet, changing wall, missing rope safety, extra limbs, dangerous equipment misuse, brand logos, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Technique plausibility, rope/harness safety, hold continuity, body topology, and gravity-driven swing.

**Use it for:** A fast cycling shot with readable line choice, wheel contact, and weather physics.

**Mode:** Rider/bicycle image + track image + cornering reference video  
**Format:** 2.39:1, target 8 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = fictional rider and exact bicycle; Image 2 = circuit corner; Video 1 = lean angle and speed only.

```text
Generate one continuous wet-circuit cycling shot. Preserve the adult rider and bicycle from Image 1: white helmet, amber glasses, navy kit, matte silver frame, black wheels, red bottle. Use the left-hand corner, curb, and barrier positions from Image 2.

Camera tracks low and parallel from outside the corner. The rider enters from screen-right, brakes before turn-in, releases the brakes, leans smoothly left at the safe angle shown in Video 1, follows the dry line, and accelerates out toward screen-left. Tires remain in contact; a narrow spray follows the rear wheel and reduces as the bike straightens. The camera does not cross the rider's path or reverse screen direction.

Overcast light, wet asphalt reflections, realistic wheel rotation and fabric movement. Sound intent: tire hiss, chain, wind, distant track ambience. Avoid motorcycle speed, sliding without correction, changing frame geometry, disappearing pedals, unsafe traffic, crash spectacle, sponsor logos, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Wheel contact, cornering sequence, bicycle geometry, screen direction, spray scale, and safe framing.

## ACT-003 Table Tennis Rally in One Take

**Use conditions / 使用条件:** Confirm a compatible route / 需先确认平台支持。Prepare the listed references and verify duration and input roles; do not treat multiple references as a first/last-frame pair. This is not ready to paste unchanged into the free tool. [Guide / 说明](../../docs/recipe-usage.md).

**Use it for:** A compact indoor sports piece where ball trajectory and player positions stay legible.

**Mode:** Two player references + court image + rally reference video  
**Format:** 9:16, target 10 seconds  
**Reference map:** Images 1–2 = fictional adult players; Image 3 = table/court; Video 1 = rally timing only.

```text
Create a vertical one-take table tennis rally in the small community gym from Image 3. Player A from Image 1 remains on the far side in a burgundy shirt; Player B from Image 2 remains near side in a cream shirt. Preserve faces, handedness, rackets, table markings, net height, and positions.

Camera begins just behind Player B's right shoulder and slides laterally without crossing the table axis. The white ball follows one readable sequence: B forehand to A backhand, A returns cross-court, B steps left for a controlled topspin, A blocks down the line, B reaches and finishes with a soft angled shot. The ball bounces exactly once on each side per legal return and remains visible; both players recover naturally after every stroke.

Sound intent: five distinct paddle contacts, shoe squeak, ball bounce, room echo. Avoid multiplying balls, impossible ricochets, changing handedness, warped rackets, body collisions, dramatic diving, score text, logos, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Ball visibility and bounce count, player sides, handedness, camera axis, and recovery footwork.
