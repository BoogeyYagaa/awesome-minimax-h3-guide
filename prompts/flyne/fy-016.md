多参考角色隔离测试

**Origin:** Flyne AI · MIT · **Status:** conceptual, not tested

**Category:** evaluation · **Task:** ref2va · **Target:** 6 seconds, 16:9

**Reference map:** Image 1 = ceramic mug geometry; Image 2 = striped wall style only; Video 1 = camera orbit only.

**Use conditions / 使用条件:** Confirm a compatible route / 需先确认平台支持。Prepare the listed references and verify duration and input roles; do not treat multiple references as a first/last-frame pair. This is not ready to paste unchanged into the free tool. [Guide / 说明](../../docs/recipe-usage.md).

## Copy-ready prompt

```text
Generate a six-second controlled studio shot of the ceramic mug from Image 1. Borrow only the background stripe palette from Image 2, never its objects or text. Borrow only the slow clockwise camera orbit from Video 1, never its subject or sound. Keep the mug stationary and geometrically identical throughout. Stop the orbit for the final second. Neutral soft light and silence. Do not merge reference subjects, add stripes onto the mug, inherit a soundtrack or invent lettering.
```

**Acceptance:** Score geometry, style placement and camera direction separately; reject cross-reference leakage.

**Recovery:** Remove one reference at a time to identify the source of leakage.

**Production value:** Provides a repeatable diagnostic brief for comparing reference-following behavior.

The duration is a creative target, not an API parameter guarantee. Select a compatible route in the [workflow guide](../../docs/workflows.md), record results with the [run template](../../templates/run-record.json), and verify input rights before use.
