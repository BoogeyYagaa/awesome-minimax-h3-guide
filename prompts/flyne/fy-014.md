# FY-014 Local craft macro process

地方工艺微距记录概念

**Origin:** Flyne AI · MIT · **Status:** conceptual, not tested

**Category:** craft · **Task:** ref2va · **Target:** 8 seconds, 1:1

**Reference map:** Video 1 = maker-approved actual hand technique; Image 1 = licensed workpiece.

## Copy-ready prompt

```text
Create a macro interpretation of the maker’s single verified brushstroke from Video 1 on the workpiece in Image 1. Begin with the brush just above the surface for one second, follow the stroke for five seconds, then hold the completed mark for two seconds. The tool angle, stroke direction and hand posture follow the demonstration. Keep the camera on the contact point with shallow but sufficient depth of field. Gentle brush sound only. No invented cultural symbols, extra strokes, finished artwork appearing instantly or claims of documentary authenticity.
```

**Acceptance:** Maker approves technique and cultural context; label the result as a generated interpretation.

**Recovery:** Use the authentic process footage if the hand or tool contact is wrong.

**Production value:** Supports a craft story while retaining maker authority over the represented technique.

The duration is a creative target, not an API parameter guarantee. Select a compatible route in the [workflow guide](../../docs/workflows.md), record results with the [run template](../../templates/run-record.json), and verify input rights before use.
