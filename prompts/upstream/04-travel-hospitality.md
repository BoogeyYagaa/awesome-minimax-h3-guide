## TRV-001 Rain-Washed Canal Town Morning

**Use it for:** A slow destination film that sells atmosphere through a believable local routine.

**Mode:** Location references + optional ambient audio  
**Format:** 16:9, target 12–15 seconds  
**Reference map:** Images 1–3 = verified architecture, bridge shape, and canal color; Audio 1 = rain and street ambience only.

```text
Create an early-morning destination film in the verified canal town shown in Images 1–3. Preserve the white plaster walls, charcoal roof tiles, narrow stone bridge, and canal width. Do not invent monuments, signs, festivals, or historical claims.

Opening: locked wide shot after light rain; water drops fall from roof edges and a shopkeeper opens plain wooden shutters. Middle: camera moves at walking pace along the canal behind a local bicycle carrying two empty woven baskets; wheels create small ripples through a shallow puddle. Ending: gentle bridge-level pan as a small boat passes under the arch and morning steam rises from an unbranded food stall in the distance.

Use soft overcast light, damp stone texture, muted green reflections, and natural human pace. Sound intent: roof drips, bicycle bell once, water against wood, quiet conversation without legible dialogue. Avoid tourist crowds, fake signage, altered architecture, costume stereotypes, drone-speed movement, saturated fantasy colors, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Geographic fidelity, respectful local depiction, water physics, architecture continuity, and no fabricated facts.

## TRV-002 Volcanic Island Guesthouse Reveal

**Use conditions / 使用条件:** Confirm a compatible route / 需先确认平台支持。Prepare the listed references and verify duration and input roles; do not treat multiple references as a first/last-frame pair. This is not ready to paste unchanged into the free tool. [Guide / 说明](../../docs/recipe-usage.md).

**Use it for:** A hospitality video that connects room design to the surrounding landscape.

**Mode:** Room image + landscape image + optional floor-plan reference  
**Format:** 16:9, target 10 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = guest room; Image 2 = verified coastline; Image 3 = spatial layout only.

```text
Produce a calm guesthouse reveal for a fictional property on a volcanic island. Preserve the room from Image 1: limewashed walls, low oak bed, woven headboard, black-stone floor, linen curtains. Use Image 2 only for the dark lava coast and pale ocean horizon visible outside. Respect the doorway and window positions in Image 3.

Begin close on a brass room key placed on the oak shelf. Camera glides backward as the door opens, revealing the bed and window in one continuous move; curtains respond softly to sea air. Continue past the bed at human walking height and finish at the open terrace, where two empty chairs face the coastline. No people are needed.

Lighting transitions naturally from warm interior bounce to cool exterior daylight. Sound intent: key touch, door latch, linen movement, distant surf. Avoid changing room geometry, adding a pool or amenities, impossible ocean placement, over-wide lens distortion, promotional text, rating claims, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Floor-plan coherence, indoor/outdoor exposure, furnishing persistence, and truthful amenity depiction.

## TRV-003 First-Person Night Market Wayfinding

**Use conditions / 使用条件:** Confirm a compatible route / 需先确认平台支持。Prepare the listed references and verify duration and input roles; do not treat multiple references as a first/last-frame pair. This is not ready to paste unchanged into the free tool. [Guide / 说明](../../docs/recipe-usage.md).

**Use it for:** A mobile travel clip focused on navigation, food discovery, and accessible pacing.

**Mode:** Market images + route sketch + optional ambience  
**Format:** 9:16, target 12 seconds  
**Reference map:** Images 1–2 = verified market look; Image 3 = route and stall positions; Audio 1 = ambience only.

```text
Create a first-person vertical walk through the evening market shown in Images 1–2. Follow the simple route in Image 3: entrance lanterns → fruit stall → steamed snack stall → small open seating area. Do not invent readable vendor names or prices.

The camera moves at a comfortable walking pace with realistic body stabilization, never rushing through people. At the fruit stall, pause as a vendor weighs three oranges; at the snack stall, a bamboo lid lifts and steam briefly crosses the lens without obscuring the path; end by turning into the seating area where one empty table is visible. Keep the path navigable and maintain consistent stall order.

Use mixed warm stall light and cool evening ambient light, correct skin tones, and restrained exposure adjustment. Sound intent: soft crowd bed, scale click, steam release, utensils. Avoid face duplication, collision with people, illegible close-up text, exoticizing details, impossible route jumps, intrusive filming, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Route continuity, crowd ethics, readable pacing, exposure shifts, and no invented signage.
